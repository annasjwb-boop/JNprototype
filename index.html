What these numbers mean

Total-loss rebuild cost is one number for your home — what a licensed contractor would charge to rebuild it all at today's prices. It never changes by disaster, and it's the number your policy limit should match: insurers require coverage near full rebuild cost, and carrying less shrinks payouts even on small claims.

Estimated damage is what changes by peril: a severe wildfire can take the whole house, while a severe winter storm or hailstorm usually wrecks the roof and systems, not everything. The dome shows the severe-scenario damage for the peril you picked — and your hardening work shrinks it.

Covered is funding that would actually pay for this specific disaster: only policies that respond to this peril count (a flood policy doesn't pay for wind damage), after your deductible, plus savings you've told us about.

Gap is the share of the rebuild with no funding source yet. Federal aid isn't counted here — it only turns on with a presidential declaration (~5% of disasters) and fills gaps after insurance.
